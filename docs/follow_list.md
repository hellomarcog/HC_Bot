# X follow list (@SuburbanDscipln)

Followed **by hand**, not by the bot — X restricts automated following, and a small, deliberate
list makes the account look like a real fan rather than an empty bot.

How to use:
- Search each name on X and confirm it's the official account (verified, linked from the band/label's own site, posting recently). Skip dead accounts and ones that mostly post politics.
- 5–10 follows a day over the first two weeks, classic bands and labels first. Target ~30–60 total, then add the odd one when you come across it.
- Don't follow private individuals, follow-for-follow accounts, or auto-follow-back. Following back real fans by hand is fine.
- Tick items off as you go (`[x]`).
